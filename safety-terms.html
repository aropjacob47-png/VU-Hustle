Safety and Terms

Safety tips

  • Meet on campus, in a place where other people are around.
  • Check the seller's reviews and photos before you buy.
  • Check the product before you pay.
  • Report any seller who sells fake products or behaves badly, using the Report this seller link on their profile.

Terms of use

  • VU Hustle is a directory. It connects buyers and sellers but does not take part in any sale or payment.
  • Sellers must show fixed prices in UGX, sell genuine products and keep one listing per business.
  • The site team approves every listing before it appears. The team can reject listings and hide or delete reviews and reports that are fake or abusive.
  • Reviews must be honest and respectful.

Privacy notice

This notice is written to follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

  • What we keep: seller names, phone numbers and photos, business details, reviewer names and comments, and messages sent through the Contact page.
  • Why we keep it: to show the directory, let buyers contact sellers, check listings and reviews, and answer questions.
  • Who can see it: seller business details, phone numbers and photos are public once a listing is approved. Contact messages are seen only by the site team.
  • Your choices: you can ask the site team to correct or remove your listing or review through the Contact page.
