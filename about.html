About VU Hustle

Why VU Hustle exists

Many students at Victoria University Uganda run small businesses selling perfumes, cakes, clothes and phone accessories. They walk around campus looking for buyers and often meet people who doubt their products. Posts in study groups can get sellers blocked, and WhatsApp statuses only reach people who already have their number.

Buyers have the same problem from the other side. They do not know who sells what right next to them.

VU Hustle gives student sellers one open place to be found and trusted. Fixed prices in UGX and reviews from other students help buyers decide with confidence.

Three simple steps

1

List

Sellers add their business with photos, prices and contact details.

2

Browse

Buyers search by category or keyword and read reviews.

3

Contact

Buyers call or use WhatsApp to reach the seller.
